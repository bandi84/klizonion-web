KLIZONION is an experimental language model project.
KLIZONION Builder is an engineering system for creating software.
The model learns by predicting the next token from a sequence of tokens.
Training uses a transformer with causal self attention.
The training loop measures loss and saves checkpoints.
A checkpoint allows training to continue later.
Good engineering means measuring results before making changes.
The Builder can eventually connect model training with automated experiments.
This is a training smoke test, not a production dataset.
